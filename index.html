Why Radici di Puglia

What makes us different

01 🌱

A Real Working Farm — Not a Tourist Show

Donatello's family, Azienda Agricola Pietra Santa, is a generations-old working farm. When you visit, you see real production — olive oil actually being pressed, cheese genuinely being made. No actors, no scripts.

vs. staged "agriturismo" experiences sold on booking platforms
02 🏠

Born & Raised Here — Not a Hired Guide

Donatello grew up in Carovigno and knows every hidden path, every farmer, every secret cove. Giovanni lived abroad for years and came back with fresh eyes. Local depth meets traveller perspective.

vs. professional guides based in Bari or Brindisi
03 🚌

The Van That Becomes a Memory

Our vintage Fiat Panorama "Peace & Love" is iconic. Rolling through Puglia in this van is itself an experience you'll talk about for years — before you even arrive at the masseria.

vs. generic minibuses or tourist coaches
04 🗺️

Countryside + Sea in One Tour

Most operators choose one or the other. We combine ancient olive groves with the hidden Adriatic coastline — including Torre Guaceto, one of Italy's most protected marine reserves.

vs. single-focus tours (only farm, or only coast)
05 ✂️

Truly Tailored — Every Group Is Unique

We don't run a fixed programme. Tell us what you love — the sea, the food, photography, local history — and we build the afternoon around you. Maximum 6–7 guests means you're never just a number.

vs. fixed bus tours for 15–20 strangers
06 ☕

Picci Bar — Already a Local Icon

Giovanni's Picci Bar is a recognised landmark of Carovigno. This gives Radici di Puglia a real presence, a place to meet and gather, and a reputation already trusted by travellers visiting the area.

vs. operators with no local base or storefront
The Experience

Not a tourist route.
A local secret.

We take you off the beaten path — to the masserie that have been producing olive oil and cheese for centuries, to coastlines that don't appear on any map, to flavours that can only be found here.

Every tour is shaped around you. Tell us what you love — the sea, the food, the animals, the ancient stones — and we'll build the perfect afternoon.

Food Tasting

Who We Are

Two friends, one deep love for Puglia

Giovanni Del Prete
Co-founder · Host & Guide
☕ Also: owner of Picci Bar, Carovigno

An engineering graduate who chose the world over a desk. Giovanni spent years working in hospitality across continents — absorbing cultures, flavours, and ways of welcoming strangers. Now he's back in Carovigno, and his mission is simple: share the region he loves with travellers ready to see it through a local's eyes.

Donatello Leobilla
Co-founder · Local Expert & Farmer
🌿 Azienda Agricola Pietra Santa

Born and raised in Carovigno, Donatello is part of Azienda Agricola Pietra Santa — a family farm with generations of roots in this land. He knows every hidden path, every ancient olive tree, every farmer who still makes cheese the old way. The land isn't just his home — it's his identity.
